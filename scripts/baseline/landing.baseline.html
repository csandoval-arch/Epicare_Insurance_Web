F.A.Q.

Frequently Asked Questions

0101

Which carriers are available?

130+ carrier appointments across Life, Health and Supplementary — national and regional. The list grows as we add appointments.

0202

Do you own my book of business?

No. You keep 100% ownership of your book from day one. Epicare is your operating infrastructure, not the owner of your work.

0303

How much does the platform cost?

GO AMS, GO CRM, Eppigo™ and Academy are included in your producer contract, with no platform fee. The only thing agreed at contracting is your commission split.

0404

Do I need to be in Florida?

No. We are headquartered in Miami, but we operate across all 52 jurisdictions: the 50 states, Washington DC and Puerto Rico. You sell wherever you are licensed.

0505

Is Epicare a startup or an established company?

Epicare Insurance Corp has been operating since 2021, with 6,000+ insured members and 100+ active agents. EIN 87-1093490 · NPN 19985316 · EPICARE® registered with the USPTO (Reg. 8148738).

0606

What if I already have my own CRM?

Keep using it. GO AMS is where your contracts, commissions and licensing live — that does not get duplicated in another system. Your data is exportable whenever you need it.

0707

How long does full onboarding take?

24 to 48 hours from signing if your licenses are active. Carrier appointments depend on each carrier turnaround; you track the live status from the portal.
